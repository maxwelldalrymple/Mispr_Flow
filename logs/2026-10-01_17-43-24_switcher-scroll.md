# App switcher: scrolling

**Branch:** `speaker-id`

| Say | Does |
|---|---|
| "scroll down" / "scroll up" | ~450 px, trackpad-style, under the pointer |
| "… a little" / "… a bit" / "… more" / "… a lot" | ×0.35 / ×0.35 / ×2 / ×3 |
| "scroll down 3 times" | repeats it |
| "page down" / "page up" | ~900 px |
| "scroll to the top" / "scroll all the way to the bottom" | ⌘↑ / ⌘↓ in the app in front |

- Scrolling uses scroll-wheel events in 120 px steps for smoothness. They go to whatever is under the mouse pointer, like a trackpad.
- A bare "up" or "down" isn't a scroll; it's treated as an app name.
