# Gemma terms on the setup Models page

**Why:** security audit finding 13. Gemma's licence requires that people who receive the model are given its Terms of Use. Until now the terms were mentioned only in the README.

**What changed:**
- The setup Models page now says "By downloading Gemma you agree to Google's **Gemma Terms of Use**".
- The link opens https://ai.google.dev/gemma/terms (`GEMMA_TERMS_URL`, `_Actions.gemmaTerms_`).
- Files: `mispr/onboarding.py`, `docs/features/setup-and-permissions.md`, and the audit log (finding 13 marked fixed).

**Tests run:** `tests/test_onboarding.py`, all passed.
- **New test:** `test_model_page_links_the_gemma_terms` checks that the link opens the terms URL.
- **Goldens regenerated and checked by eye:** `setup_models_downloading`, `setup_models_error`, `setup_models_ready`.
