# App switcher: "unmute" for everything

**Branch:** `speaker-id`

- "unmute" (the sound) and "unmute mic" already worked.
- **Change:** tab muting is now directional instead of a toggle.
  - "mute tab" presses only "Mute site"; "unmute tab" presses only "Unmute site".
  - If the tab is already in the asked-for state, the menu is closed again and the widget says "… is already muted / unmuted".
  - This also covers "unmute Spotify" and "unmute the site in Chrome".
