# Meeting notes: no more duplicated lines from speaker echo

**Branch:** `speaker-id`

## Problem (user screenshot, 2026-10-01 12:28)

With the call playing through the Mac's speakers instead of headphones, the mic hears the other side again. Each of their sentences showed twice: once as "Male 1" from system audio, and again as "You" from the mic, e.g. "CICD on Google Next and GitOps on KubeCon. Does that sound right?".

## Fix (`Echo` in `MisprCore/LiveMeeting.swift`, used by `LiveTranscript`)

- Mic text is compared, as words (lowercase, letters and digits only, so "CI/CD" = "CICD" and "re:Invent" matches), with what the other side said within **±10 s**.
- Shared runs of **3 or more words** are echo. A whole short mic line of **2 or more words** that matches is echo too. A single shared word ("Yeah.") is kept, because you may really have said it.
- If at least **60%** of the mic line is echo, or fewer than 2 of your own words are left, the line is dropped. Otherwise only the echoed words are cut out, so talking over the echo keeps your part.
- It works whichever arrives first. A late "them" line re-checks nearby mic lines and removes the copies already shown.
- Live (grey) mic previews that are echo are hidden too.
- With headphones there is no echo, so nothing changes.

Apple's voice-processing echo canceller wasn't used: on macOS it ducks other apps' audio, which would lower the call you're listening to.
