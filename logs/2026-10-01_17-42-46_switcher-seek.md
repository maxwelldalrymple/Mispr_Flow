# App switcher: skip forward/back N seconds

**Branch:** `speaker-id`

| Say | Does |
|---|---|
| "skip forward 30 seconds", "jump ahead 2 minutes" | presses → once per 5 s (6 and 24 presses) |
| "skip back 15 seconds", "rewind 20 seconds", "skip backward thirty seconds" | presses ← once per 5 s |
| "skip forward" / "rewind" (no time) | 10 s |
| "… in Chrome" | brings the app forward first |
| "go back 10 seconds" | skip back (it has a time); plain "go back" / "go forward" stays the browser's back/forward |

- **Why arrow keys:** macOS has no system-wide "seek N seconds". Arrow keys skip 5 s in YouTube, most web players, QuickTime and IINA.
- **Players with a different step** move by their own amount per press; Netflix, for example, uses 10 s.
- Times round to the nearest 5 s, with a minimum of one press.
